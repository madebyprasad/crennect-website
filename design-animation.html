Design & Animation

Design is the brand promise people see first. Animation is what makes them stay. We make visual work that feels premium without trying too hard.

AI made generic design free. Bespoke craft is the only signal of seriousness left.

Why this matters now

Three forces are putting design back at the centre of how brands compete for attention.

  • Templates have flooded the market. Anyone can spin up a clean landing page in an afternoon. Looking professional is no longer enough. Looking distinctive is the new bar.
  • First impressions decide most decisions. A founder, a buyer, or an investor decides whether you are serious within the first three seconds of seeing your site. Design is the only thing that loads in those three seconds.
  • Motion is the new still. Static design used to win. Now it is the baseline. Brands that show up with thoughtful motion and animation feel a generation ahead.

Our approach in 5 phases

Crennect runs design and animation as a craft process. Small team, deep involvement, every detail considered.

1

Brief and references (Week 1)

We collect references, look at competitors, audit the current design, and align on the visual direction in plain language before any pixel moves.

2

Direction (Week 2)

We propose two to three creative directions with mood boards, type pairings, colour ranges, and motion principles. You pick one, we refine.

3

Concept design (Weeks 3 to 4)

We design the core artefacts. Logo and identity, key page layouts, primary illustrations, motion principles, and the design grammar that holds the rest together.

4

Production and motion (Weeks 4 to 6)

We produce the full asset set. Illustrations, motion graphics, packaging, social templates, web pages, and any animation that needs production-grade work.

5

Handover and guidelines (Week 6)

We hand over editable files, a concise visual guidelines document, motion principles, and a working session with your in-house designers so they can run with it.

What changes after we are done

Good design changes more than the website. It changes how your team works and how your customers perceive you. The patterns we see:

  • Your homepage starts converting because the design supports the message, not fighting it.
  • New hires onboard faster because the brand is now visually documented.
  • Sales decks stop looking like Google Slides templates.
  • Premium customers start showing up because the visual quality signals you are serious.
  • Your team can move faster on content because the design grammar is now decided.

Who this is built for

Design and animation work is the right move when at least one of these is true for you.

  • Your brand was designed when the company was a quarter of its current size, and it is starting to feel like it.
  • You are launching a new product, sub-brand, or category line and need a complete visual set.
  • You are entering a premium segment and your current visual quality does not match the ambition.
  • You have a strong brand strategy and need execution to match it.
  • You are scaling your in-house design team and need a foundation they can build on.

Questions founders ask before we start

How long does a design engagement take?

Most engagements run six to ten weeks for a complete identity and asset set. A focused engagement (website redesign only, or packaging only) can run three to four weeks. A full identity, web, and animation engagement can take twelve.

Will we get editable files we can work from later?

Yes. Final files are delivered in editable formats your team can open in Figma, Illustrator, After Effects, or whatever tools you use. We also leave a concise visual guidelines document.

Do you work with our existing in-house designers?

Yes, and we prefer it. The work lands better when your designers are part of the process. We run weekly working sessions and hand off in a form your team can extend.

Can you handle web design and development together?

Design yes, development we partner on. For web, we design and prototype in detail, then either work with your dev team or our development partners to build. We project-manage the build closely.

Do you do logo redesigns on their own?

Sometimes, when the rest of the identity is already strong. More often we recommend doing the logo as part of a broader identity refresh because a new logo on old visual grammar usually looks awkward.

What if we already have a brand identity we like?

Even better. We can come in at the production stage, design specific assets within your existing identity, and not touch the core brand. Many engagements start that way.

Do you do motion and animation in-house?

Yes. Motion direction, kinetic typography, logo motion, product animation, and explainer animation are all handled by our in-house team. For very complex 3D or VFX work we partner with specialist studios.
