Content Production

Content production is the work of turning brand intent into things people actually want to watch, read, or share. We make work that holds attention longer than the scroll lets it.

Volume is now infinite. The brands that win are the ones whose work people choose to finish.

Why this matters now

The economics of content have shifted in three ways founders we work with are still adjusting to.

  • Cost of production has collapsed. Anyone can make a film in a weekend. Quality has stopped being about the gear. It is about taste, casting, art direction, and a sharp creative idea.
  • Volume is the default, not the wedge. Brands are pushing more content than ever. The brands that stand out are the ones whose work is consistently watchable, not just consistently posted.
  • AI raises the floor but cannot replace taste. You can spin up a hundred variations in an afternoon. Choosing the right one and giving it a brand voice is still a human job. Production is now about the editorial layer.

Our approach in 5 phases

Crennect treats content production as a craft process, not an assembly line. The team you brief on day one is the team that delivers on day forty.

1

Creative brief (Week 1)

We turn the business outcome into a creative brief. Audience, tone, format, channel, distribution plan, and the one thing this piece of work has to communicate.

2

Pre-production and treatment (Week 2)

Treatment, mood, shotlist, casting, location scout, and music direction. By the end of this phase you can feel the final work before we shoot it.

3

Shoot or capture (Week 3)

On-location or studio shoot, photography, voice recording, motion capture, whatever the format needs. Crennect handles or coordinates the crew, talent, and locations.

4

Edit and post (Weeks 4 to 5)

Picture edit, colour grade, sound design, music, motion, and any AI-led enhancement. Two rounds of feedback baked in.

5

Delivery and adaptations (Week 6)

Final master delivered in every aspect ratio and format you need. Vertical reels, square posts, horizontal landings, twenty-second shorts, full-length film.

What changes after we are done

Content production done right does not just give you assets. It changes how your marketing and sales teams operate. The patterns we see:

  • A library that lasts a full quarter without your team scrambling for the next post.
  • Lower cost per useful asset, because every shoot day yields twenty pieces of work, not two.
  • A sharper brand voice that is recognisable across every place your content shows up.
  • Paid performance lifts because the creative is genuinely good, not just on-brief.
  • Sales teams start sending the content to prospects unprompted, because it actually helps them sell.

Who this is built for

Content production is the right move when at least one of these is true for you.

  • You are launching a new product and need a full asset library by launch date.
  • You are scaling marketing and your team cannot keep up with the volume of asks.
  • You are building authority in your category and need a recognisable visual voice.
  • You are refreshing your brand library after a repositioning or identity update.
  • You have a one-time event, fundraise, or partnership announcement that needs a hero film.

Questions founders ask before we start

How many assets do we get from a typical engagement?

A standard four-to-six week production yields one hero film, two to three short films, fifteen to twenty social cuts, and a photography library of fifty to a hundred frames. Exact yield depends on scope.

Do you handle talent, locations, and crew?

Yes. Crennect manages talent casting, location scouting, crew, equipment, and post. You see one invoice, one timeline, and one team.

Can we shoot multiple campaigns in one go?

Yes. Many brands we work with consolidate two to three quarterly campaigns into a single shoot week to bring per-asset cost down. We plan the shoot list to make this efficient.

Who owns the footage and raw files?

You own the final delivered files. Raw files are archived with us and available on request at a small handover cost, subject to the talent and music licenses we negotiated for that shoot.

Do you work with AI-generated content?

Yes, where it serves the work. AI is a useful tool for ideation, low-cost variations, and certain motion effects. We do not use AI for any work that needs real human casting, real product, or brand-critical hero content unless agreed in advance.

How fast can you turn around a single film?

A standalone short film with no big-shoot requirement can be made in two to three weeks. A larger hero film needs four to six. We will be honest about the timeline before we start.

What if we already have a creative idea?

Even better. We can come in at the production stage and execute. Many of our engagements start that way and grow into bigger creative partnerships once the first piece lands.
