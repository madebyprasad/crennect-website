Brand Autopsies

Everyone studies winners.
We do the post-mortems.

Remember that brand you loved five years ago and haven't thought about since? Somebody should ask what happened to it. That's what we do here.

What's a brand autopsy, exactly?

When a brand dies, or quietly stops mattering, everyone has a theory and nobody checks. An autopsy is us checking. Every episode follows the same honest procedure: what happened (the story, told properly), the decline timeline (where it actually started going wrong; it's never where you think), the cause of death (one line, no hedging), and what they should have done, so you don't repeat it. Business schools study these failures years later. We do it while the lessons are still warm.

Case File №1
featured autopsy

The brand that solved the wrong problem

Not killed by competitors. Not killed by funding. Killed by a pattern so predictable you can see it coming, if you know which layer of the market you actually live in.

The rise

A real pain, a fast solution, early loyalty. Everything looks unbreakable.

The drift

The business optimises the implementation, the "how", while the customer's real job quietly moves up a layer.

The wave

Technology eats the bottom of the pyramid first. Lower problems vanish. The category doesn't shrink. It evaporates.

Time of death

Declared irrelevant. Customers didn't leave angry. They just stopped needing it.

Cause of death

Solved the lower problem. Ignored the core one.
