Privacy Policy.

What we collect when you use Breaking Brands, why, and what we do with it. Kept short and plain.

Effective date: 8 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Crennect LLP ("Crennect", "we", "us", "our") handles information you share when you use Breaking Brands at crennect.com/breakingbrands (the "Site"). It complements Crennect's main Privacy Policy. By using the Site or sharing your information with us, you agree to this Policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter; your name, email, brand or company details and message when you apply (get featured, build your brand, join The Circle); and anything you share when you email us.
  • Information collected automatically: basic device and usage data such as IP address, browser, operating system, pages visited, and how you reached the Site, collected through Google Analytics.
  • On-site interactions: choices you make in features such as the Prediction Arena and Heat Board votes, generally on an aggregated or pseudonymous basis.

3. How we use your information

To send you the newsletter; to review and respond to applications; to operate The Circle and Site features; to understand and improve how the Site is used; to communicate with you; and to meet legal obligations.

4. Consent and your choices

When you subscribe, apply, or otherwise share your information, you consent to our use of it as described here. You can unsubscribe or withdraw your consent at any time by writing to reach@crennect.com. Doing so won't affect anything we lawfully did with your data beforehand.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies, primarily through Google Analytics, to understand usage and improve the Site. Embedded YouTube videos and Google Fonts are served by Google and may set cookies or log your IP address when loaded or played. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some may affect functionality.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Site and communications (for example, Google Analytics, our email and hosting providers), and where required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Third-party embeds and links

The Site embeds and links to third-party services such as YouTube and Google. Your interactions with them are governed by their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

8. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as it's useful for the purposes above (for example, while you're subscribed), unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

9. Data security

We care about keeping your information safe and we actively work to protect it. At the same time, Breaking Brands is run by a small, early-stage team without enterprise-scale resources, and no website or online service can ever be fully secure. We therefore can't guarantee the security of your information, and we don't accept liability for any loss, misuse, or unauthorised access, including where it results from events outside our reasonable control. You share your information with us at your own risk.

10. Where your data is processed

Some of our service providers (such as Google) may process data on servers outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and technical safeguards.

11. Children

Breaking Brands is intended for a professional adult audience and is not directed to children under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted here with its effective date.

13. Contact

For any question about this Policy, or to unsubscribe, contact us at reach@crennect.com. Crennect's main Privacy Policy also applies.
