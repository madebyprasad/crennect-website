What is inside

The canvas walks you through eight focused boxes. Each one answers a question that, once clear, makes the next twelve months of marketing decisions easier.

1

Who are you for

The specific person or role you serve. Not "everyone in the category", not "modern consumers", a real person you can picture.

2

The alternative they use today

What they currently do instead of you. This is your real competition, which is often not who you think it is.

3

The market category

The frame your customer uses to evaluate you. Get this wrong and you compete with the wrong people on the wrong terms.

4

The trigger moment

The specific pain or aspiration that makes someone start looking for you. The before-and-after that gets them moving.

5

Your wedge

The single sharp way you are different from the alternatives. Not three differences, one. Pick the one that matters most.

6

The proof

Evidence the wedge is real. Customer outcomes, data, a recognisable client, your founder story. Pick the proof that is hardest to fake.

7

What you are choosing against

The customers, products, and revenue you are saying no to. A position that does not name what it is giving up is not yet a position.

8

Your one-sentence position

The final line. Boil the previous seven boxes into one sentence your team and your customers can repeat. If you cannot, none of it is sharp enough yet.

How to use it

  • Block 30 minutes. Print the canvas. Sit alone first, even if you plan to do it with your team later. The honest version comes out faster solo.
  • Fill in pencil first. The first answer is rarely the final one. Give yourself room to revise.
  • Do not skip box 7. Naming what you are choosing against is the hardest box and the most useful one.
  • Test the one-sentence position. Read it to five customers, five investors, and three new hires. If they can repeat it in their own words, you have a position. If they cannot, it needs another pass.
  • Come back to it. Most teams revise after a week. The point is to get the conversation started, not to finish the canvas in one go.

Frequently asked

What is the Brand Positioning Canvas?

A one-page worksheet that helps you sharpen your brand position by answering eight focused questions. Designed to be filled in by a founder or a leadership team in about thirty minutes, alone or together.

Who is this canvas for?

Founders, CMOs, brand leads, and marketing teams who need a sharper sense of what their brand stands for. Most useful for brands that are scaling, repositioning, launching a new product, or feeling that the message is no longer landing the way it used to.

How long does it take to fill in?

About thirty minutes for a first pass. Most teams revisit it after a week to refine the answers. The first version is rarely the final one. The point is to get the conversation started.

Do I need to share my email to download it?

No. The canvas is free and the download is direct. We do not gate it behind a form.

Can I share or use it with my team?

Yes. Share it with your team, your board, your investors. Print it out and pin it to a wall. Just keep the Crennect attribution on the worksheet so others can find the source.

What do I do after I fill it in?

Test it. Run the one-sentence position past five customers, five investors, and a few new hires. If they can repeat it back to you in their own words, the position is sharp. If they cannot, it needs another pass. We are happy to look at your filled-in canvas and help you tighten it if you want.

Want help tightening your position?

If you have filled in the canvas and want a second opinion, we are happy to look at it. A fifteen-minute call is usually enough to tell whether your position is sharp or whether there is one more round of work to do.
