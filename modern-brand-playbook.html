What is inside

The playbook is built around a single idea: every modern brand is built on five layers, stacked in order. Each layer rests on the one below it. If a layer is missing or weak, every layer above it gets distorted.

1

Positioning

Who you are for, what you stand for, what you choose not to stand for. The upstream decision every other brand choice rests on.

2

Narrative and messaging

The story your team can tell without you in the room. Voice, tone, headline frameworks, and the reusable templates that keep everyone in sync.

3

Visual identity

The grammar that makes your brand recognisable in a half-second. Logo, type, colour, motion, photography and the rules that hold them together.

4

Activation

Where strategy meets the actual world. Channel playbooks, content cadence, sales enablement, hiring pages, approval flows.

5

AI capability

Production-grade AI woven into creative workflows. Volume goes up, brand consistency does not break.

Also inside

  • A 90-day playbook. A step-by-step plan to work through all five layers with your team in three months.

Frequently asked

What is the Modern Brand Playbook?

A free 12-page guide that walks through the five layers every modern brand needs: positioning, narrative, identity, activation, and AI capability. Each layer gets its own chapter with a test you can run on your own brand. There is also a 90-day plan and a self-assessment checklist.

Who is this playbook for?

Founders, CMOs, brand leads, and senior marketers who want a shared framework for thinking about their brand. Especially useful for teams scaling past the early stage, when the brand built at startup speed is starting to feel like the bottleneck.

How long does it take to read?

About thirty minutes to read end to end. The self-assessment and 90-day plan are the parts most teams come back to after the first read.

Do I need to share my email to download it?

No. The playbook is free and the download is direct. We do not gate it behind a form.

Can I share it with my team?

Yes. Share it with your team, your board, your investors. Just keep the Crennect attribution on the document so others can find the source.

What if I want help with one of the layers?

That is what we do. A fifteen-minute call is usually enough to tell you which layer to focus on first and what a focused engagement would look like.

Need help with a specific layer?

If the playbook surfaced a layer you want to work on, we are happy to look at where you are and tell you honestly what a focused engagement would look like.
