AI Capabilities

AI is not replacing creative teams. It is letting good creative teams ship ten times more, ten times sharper. We build the AI capabilities that turn your creative team into a force.

AI is the multiplier on good taste. Without taste, more AI just means more noise.

Why this matters now

AI has changed creative economics in three ways most brands are still slow to use.

  • Volume is now free. Brands that used to ship one campaign a quarter can now ship one a week. The constraint moved from production capacity to creative direction and approval cycles.
  • Personalisation is now feasible. Until last year, personalising creative for ten audience segments was a luxury. Now it is a workflow. The brands that use this well pull ahead on conversion and recall.
  • Brand consistency is a new craft. AI is great at producing variety. It is bad at staying on-brand without help. The agencies and brands that solve this layer win the next cycle.

Our approach in 5 phases

Crennect runs AI capability work as a build engagement. We audit, design, build, and train your team to keep using it. We do not hand over a one-shot prompt and call it done.

1

Audit (Weeks 1 to 2)

We look at your current creative workflows, the volume you produce, the bottlenecks, and the use cases where AI will pay off fastest.

2

Capability map (Week 2)

We propose a short list of three to five AI capabilities ranked by impact, with effort, cost, and risk for each. You pick the ones to build.

3

Workflow design (Weeks 3 to 4)

We design the workflows around the chosen capabilities. Inputs, prompts, brand guardrails, approval gates, and where humans stay in the loop.

4

Build and integrate (Weeks 5 to 7)

We build the tools. Model fine-tuning where needed, brand persona training, workflow automation, content generation tooling, chatbot integrations. We connect them to your existing stack.

5

Training and handover (Week 8)

We train your creative and marketing teams. Hands-on sessions, a living playbook, and ninety days of async support so the capability sticks after we leave.

What changes after we are done

AI capabilities are upstream investments. Once they are in place, they change how every creative cycle runs. The patterns we see:

  • Creative cycles get five to ten times faster on volume work like adaptations, variants, and translations.
  • Cost per asset drops sharply on long-tail content like product variants, regional cuts, and personalised landing pages.
  • Brand voice stays consistent across high volume because the AI is trained on it, not asked to guess.
  • Your team spends more time on direction and approval, less on grunt production work.
  • New types of content become feasible. Personalised emails, dynamic ads, interactive product experiences.

Who this is built for

AI capability work is the right move when at least one of these is true for you.

  • Your team produces high volumes of content and the production load is becoming the bottleneck.
  • You are running personalised marketing and the manual cost of variations is hurting margin.
  • You are entering new markets or languages and need consistent brand voice at scale.
  • You are a small marketing team trying to operate at enterprise volume.
  • You want to build AI into the brand operations layer before competitors do.

Questions founders ask before we start

How is this different from just using ChatGPT or Midjourney?

Off-the-shelf AI is great for individual tasks. We build the layer on top: brand-trained models, workflows, guardrails, and approval gates. The result is AI that stays on-brand reliably, not AI you have to babysit prompt by prompt.

How long does an AI capability engagement take?

A focused engagement on one or two capabilities runs six to eight weeks. A full audit, build, and team training program runs ten to twelve. We can also work on a longer retainer where we keep building capabilities as your needs evolve.

Do we need to train custom models, or can we use existing ones?

Both, depending on the use case. For brand voice and visual style we usually fine-tune. For general workflows we use commercial models with strong prompt and brand-guardrail design. We will recommend what is right for each capability.

What about data privacy and brand IP?

We design the work so your brand IP and customer data stay under your control. We use enterprise-tier models with no-training agreements where needed, and we document the data flow before any sensitive data touches a model.

Will our team be able to maintain this after you leave?

Yes. The work is not finished until your team can run it without us. Training sessions, a living playbook, and ninety days of async support are all included by default.

What if AI is moving so fast that what you build will be outdated in six months?

Two things. First, we build for capability, not for a specific tool. If a better model comes out, the workflow swaps in the new model without rebuilding everything. Second, we offer light retainers to keep capabilities updated as the underlying tools evolve.

Can you integrate AI into our existing creative tools?

Yes. We integrate with Figma, Adobe, the major DAMs, your CRM, your marketing automation, and most common stacks. The goal is for AI to live inside the tools your team already uses, not as another window to switch to.
