Creative Campaigns

A campaign is not a deliverable. It is a bet on what your audience will remember a week later. We build campaigns that earn organic recall, not just paid impressions.

A campaign worth running is one that gets repeated by your audience, not just paid for by your team.

Why this matters now

Three shifts in the market are making creative campaigns harder to land and more valuable when they do.

  • Attention is fragmented. A campaign now has to land on a reel, a podcast spot, a cinema slot, a retail shelf, and a sales deck. Every channel rewards different storytelling. Without a unifying creative thread, the spend leaks.
  • Distribution costs more than production. Producing a film is cheaper than ever. Getting people to watch it is harder than ever. The campaigns that win design distribution into the brief, not after it.
  • Creative quality is the new media buy. A sharp idea that gets shared organically beats a flat idea pushed hard with money. The math has flipped.

Our approach in 5 phases

Crennect runs creative campaigns end to end. From the first insight session to the last performance review, the team you start with is the team you finish with.

1

Insight (Week 1)

We map the audience, the cultural moment, and the white space your brand can credibly own. Sessions with leadership, three to five quick customer chats, a competitive scan, and a working hypothesis for the campaign.

2

Concept (Weeks 2 to 3)

We develop three to five creative concepts grounded in the insight. Each concept comes with a tagline, a story arc, and a sample asset so you can feel it before we build it.

3

Storyboard and assets (Weeks 3 to 4)

The chosen concept gets storyboarded across formats. Hero film, social cuts, OOH adaptations, sales-deck use, and any retail or packaging tie-ins.

4

Production (Weeks 4 to 6)

We produce. Film, photography, motion, social adaptations, copy versions. Crennect handles or coordinates the full production, including talent, locations, and post.

5

Distribution and measurement (Week 6 onwards)

We hand over the asset library with channel-specific guidance, a measurement plan, and a post-launch review at the four-week mark.

What changes after we are done

Campaigns that earn earned reach show up in places paid ads cannot reach. The patterns we see when a campaign lands:

  • Organic recall goes up. People can name the campaign without seeing the ad.
  • Share of voice in your category rises against named competitors.
  • Cost per qualified lead drops because brand pre-warms the conversation.
  • Sales teams start opening calls with the campaign idea instead of a feature.
  • The asset library outlives the media spend by three to six months.

Who this is built for

Creative campaigns are the right move when at least one of these is true for you.

  • You are launching a new product, category, or market this quarter.
  • You are repositioning the brand after a pivot, leadership change, or fundraise.
  • You are defending market share against a well-funded new entrant.
  • You have strong organic content already and want a hero campaign to anchor the year.
  • You are entering a new geography or audience segment for the first time.

Questions founders ask before we start

How long does a creative campaign engagement take?

A typical end-to-end campaign runs eight to twelve weeks from insight to launch. Production length depends on the format. A digital-first campaign can launch in six. A TV-led campaign with multiple shoot days can take fourteen.

Can you handle production, or do we need a separate production house?

We handle production end to end, including talent, location, crew, and post. For very large productions we partner with specialist studios, but we project-manage the whole thing under one brief.

What does a campaign engagement cost?

Campaigns vary by scope. A single hero film with social adaptations starts in one bracket. A full multi-channel campaign with OOH, CTV, and retail tie-ins sits in another. We will quote after the first call once we understand scope.

Will the work be ours to use forever?

Yes. Final creative belongs to you, subject to the talent and music licensing terms agreed in the contract. We retain showcase rights for our portfolio unless restricted in writing.

How do you measure campaign performance?

We agree on the metrics that matter before launch. Common ones: aided and unaided recall, share of voice, search lift, organic engagement, paid CPM, cost per qualified lead, and sales pipeline lift in the launch window.

Do you do retainer-based campaigns or one-off engagements?

Both. Many brands start with one campaign and move to a retainer after they see it land. The retainer model is usually a smaller monthly fee with a quarterly creative drop and ongoing distribution support.

What if our internal team will run distribution?

Even better. We design the campaign so your internal team can run it without us in the room. The channel playbook covers the questions your media partners and agencies will ask once the campaign is live.
