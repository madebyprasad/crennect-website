Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Crennect LLP ("Crennect", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us when you visit crennect.com (the "Website") or work with us. By using the Website or our services, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us, and some basic technical information that's collected automatically when you visit the Website.

a) What you share with us

  • Contact form submissions: Your name, email, phone number, company name, services of interest, and anything else you choose to share when you fill out our enquiry forms.
  • Newsletter and magazine subscriptions: Your email when you subscribe to our newsletter or Creative Magazine.
  • Direct communication: What you share when you email or message us at reach@crennect.com or on WhatsApp.

b) Automatically collected

  • Basic device data: IP address, browser, operating system, pages you visited, and how you reached our site.
  • Cookies and analytics: Through tools like Google Analytics, used to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

2. How we use your information

We use what you share to:

  • Reply to your enquiries and project requests;
  • Deliver the services you've engaged us for;
  • Send our newsletter, Creative Magazine, and updates you've subscribed to;
  • Improve the Website and the experience for visitors;
  • Run the day-to-day business — invoicing, internal records, and the like.

3. Your consent

By submitting a form, subscribing to our communications, or otherwise sharing your information with us, you're giving us your consent to use it as described in this Policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at reach@crennect.com — though that won't affect anything we've already done with your data before you ask us to stop.

4. Cookies and tracking

The Website uses cookies and similar tools to function and to help us understand how visitors use the site. These include:

  • Essential cookies the Website needs to work;
  • Analytics cookies like Google Analytics that help us measure traffic and performance;
  • Marketing cookies, where we run campaigns.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Website may not work as intended if you do.

5. How we share information

We don't sell your personal data. We may share it only:

  • With service providers we use to run the business — hosting, email, CRM, analytics, payment processors — who handle data on our behalf;
  • With professional advisers like our lawyers or accountants when needed;
  • When required by law, such as a court order or a request from authorities, or to protect our rights or the safety of others;
  • In a business transfer, for example if Crennect is ever merged, acquired, or restructured.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected — for example, to respond to your enquiry, deliver a project, send communications you've subscribed to, or maintain our business and accounting records. When we no longer need it, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Data security

We take reasonable care to keep your data safe and use commonly available tools to help protect it. That said, no system on the internet is fully secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If you ever notice anything off with how your data is handled, please write to us at reach@crennect.com and we'll look into it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or update it;
  • Ask us to delete it, where we don't need to keep it for valid business or record-keeping reasons;
  • Withdraw consent for any communications we send you.

To exercise any of these, just write to us at reach@crennect.com and we'll get back to you.

9. Third-party links

The Website links out to other websites and platforms — Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, Linktree, and our portfolio sub-domain, among others. This Policy doesn't cover those third parties. If you visit them, please read their own privacy practices.

10. Where your data is processed

Some of our tools and hosting partners are based outside India, so your data may be processed there. By using the Website, you understand and agree to this.

11. Get in touch

If you have any questions, requests, or concerns about your data or this Policy, just write to us. We'll do our best to help.
