Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 June 2026  |  Last updated: 2 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Crennect LLP ("Crennect", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you visit crennect.com (the "Website") or otherwise engage with our creative and digital marketing services (collectively, the "Services").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), and other applicable Indian laws.

By accessing or using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as information collected automatically when you interact with the Website.

a) Information you provide to us

  • Contact form submissions: Name, email address, phone number, company name, services of interest, and any other information you choose to share when you submit our "Start the conversation" or related enquiry forms.
  • Newsletter and magazine subscriptions: Email address (and, where you opt in, additional preferences) when you subscribe to our newsletter, "Receive cool stuff" updates, or our Creative Magazine.
  • Correspondence: Records of communication when you email, message, or otherwise correspond with us, including via reach@crennect.com or WhatsApp.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages visited, time spent on pages, clicks, and approximate location derived from IP.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: As described in Section 4.
  • Analytics data: Aggregated and pseudonymous data through tools such as Google Analytics (gtag), Vercel Speed Insights, and Google reCAPTCHA, used to measure performance, prevent abuse, and improve the Website.
We do not knowingly collect financial information, government identifiers, biometric data, health information, or other "sensitive personal data or information" as defined under the SPDI Rules through the Website. If we ever need to collect such data for a specific engagement, we will obtain your explicit written consent first.

2. How we use your information

We use your personal data for the following specified, lawful purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, project briefs, and service requests;
  • To deliver our creative, marketing, content, video, AI-led storytelling, and related Services;
  • To send our newsletter, Creative Magazine, and other communications you have opted into (with an option to unsubscribe at any time);
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Website;
  • To understand visitor behaviour and improve user experience through analytics;
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and accounting obligations;
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity;
  • To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

3. Legal basis and your consent

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data based on:

  • Your consent — freely given, specific, informed, and unconditional — provided when you submit a form, subscribe to our communications, or accept cookies; and
  • Certain legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act, including responding to a request you have voluntarily made, complying with law, and performing functions authorised by law.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at reach@crennect.com. Withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

The Website uses cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to function correctly and to help us understand how visitors use the site.

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the Website to function (e.g., page navigation, security, reCAPTCHA protection on forms).
  • Performance and analytics cookies — such as those set by Google Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights, which help us measure traffic, sources, and content performance.
  • Functionality cookies — remember your preferences.
  • Marketing cookies — used by us or our partners to deliver and measure the performance of marketing campaigns, where applicable.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Website.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers and processors: Trusted third parties who process data on our behalf — for example, web hosting (Vercel), email delivery, CRM, analytics (Google), reCAPTCHA, cloud storage, and similar providers — bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Professional advisers: Lawyers, auditors, accountants, and consultants, where reasonably necessary.
  • Legal and regulatory: Where required by law, court order, or a request from a competent authority, or to protect our rights, property, safety, or that of our clients or the public.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to applicable confidentiality safeguards.
  • With your consent: When you have specifically authorised sharing (for example, publishing a testimonial with your name and company).

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete, anonymise, or archive it in accordance with applicable law.

7. Data security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures consistent with the SPDI Rules and prevailing industry standards, including access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS), secure storage, and periodic review of safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is, however, 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If you become aware of any security vulnerability or unauthorised access concerning your data, please notify us immediately at reach@crennect.com.

8. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to applicable law, including the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken;
  • Correct, complete, or update inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • Erase personal data that is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • Withdraw consent previously given;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (see Section 12).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at reach@crennect.com. We will respond within the timelines prescribed by applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

The Website and our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it promptly. A parent or legal guardian who believes their child has provided us personal data may write to us at reach@crennect.com.

10. Third-party links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites, plugins, and platforms (including Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, Linktree, and our portfolio sub-domain). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of every website you visit.

11. International data transfers

Some of our service providers may be located outside India. Where personal data is transferred outside India, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such transfers comply with the DPDP Act and that an adequate level of protection is maintained through contractual safeguards.
